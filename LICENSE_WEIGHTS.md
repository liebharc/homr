[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode)
(CC BY-SA 4.0).

This covers the weight files that `homr --init` downloads, in every format we
publish (PyTorch, ONNX, fp16).

## Attribution

Credit the weights as:

> homr model weights by the homr contributors, https://github.com/liebharc/homr,

## Our intent

This section explains how we read the license. It adds no terms to it.

- Fine-tuned weights, and other weights based on ours, are adapted material. If
  you share them, share them under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Shipping the unchanged weights inside an app doesn't make the app adapted
  material. The app may keep its own license.
- Converting the weights to another format is allowed.
- CC BY-SA 4.0 forbids technical measures, such as encryption, that keep
  recipients from using the weights as the license allows.

## Training data

The license can't grant more rights than the training data allows. The training
datasets and their licenses are listed in [LICENSE_FAQ.md](LICENSE_FAQ.md#model-weights).

## Earlier releases

Weights released before this license change were published under the AGPL-3.0.
They are now also available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
